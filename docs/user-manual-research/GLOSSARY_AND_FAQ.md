# Simple-English Glossary and Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Document ID:** DASS-RES-07  
**Language Level:** Plain English, Zero Jargon  

---

## 1. Simple-English Glossary

- **AI (Artificial Intelligence):** Smart computer software that can look at an invoice image, understand where text is written, and pull out numbers and dates automatically, just like a human reader.
- **OCR (Optical Character Recognition):** The technology that converts pictures of words on paper or PDF files into digital text that the computer can read and search.
- **Workflow:** An automated digital assembly line that moves a document step-by-step from one manager to another until it is completely signed off.
- **Stage (e.g. Stage 1, Stage 2):** A single step in an approval process. For example, Stage 1 might be store manager review, while Stage 2 is finance payment verification.
- **Approval:** A manager's official sign-off confirming that an invoice or document is correct, valid, and ready to move forward.
- **Rejection:** The decision to turn down a document because it has errors, missing information, or unapproved expenses.
- **Put on Hold:** A temporary pause placed on a document when a manager needs more time or additional facts before deciding.
- **Compliance Checklist:** A list of mandatory yes/no questions that an approver must verify and tick before the system lets them approve an invoice.
- **Division:** A specific branch, subsidiary, or business entity within the company (e.g., HQ for Headquarters, VCC, ACC).
- **Work Tracker:** The main task board where employees and managers see all documents waiting for attention.
- **Document Key (or DocKey):** A unique tracking code (like a courier tracking number) automatically assigned to every document in the system.
- **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Security rules that ensure staff members only see the pages and documents necessary for their specific job.
- **ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning):** The company's central accounting database (like SAP or Tally). DocuFlow connects to it so staff do not have to type in approved invoices twice.

## 2. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Q1: Why can't I see the green "Approve" button on an invoice?
**Answer:** There are two common reasons:
1. The document is currently assigned to someone else at this stage. Only the assigned approver (or a system administrator) can approve it.
2. You have not ticked all required checklist boxes yet. Scroll down to the **Compliance Checklist** on the right side of the screen and make sure every box is checked.

### Q2: What happens if I make a mistake and approve the wrong invoice?
**Answer:** If the invoice has only passed Stage 1, contact the Stage 2 approver immediately and ask them to click **Send Back** or **Reject**. If the document has already reached final "Approved" status, inform your System Administrator or Finance Manager so they can halt ERP synchronization.

### Q3: Why did the system log me out while I was working?
**Answer:** DocuFlow has an automatic 20-minute inactivity timer to protect company data if you step away from your desk. The system displays a 2-minute warning countdown before logging you out. Moving your mouse or clicking anywhere resets this timer.

### Q4: Can I upload a photograph of an invoice taken with my mobile phone?
**Answer:** Yes! DocuFlow supports high-quality `.jpg` and `.png` image files as well as standard `.pdf` files up to 10 MB in size. Ensure the photograph is clear, well-lit, and all four corners of the invoice are visible.

### Q5: How do I know if an invoice has already been paid?
**Answer:** Look at the status badge in the **Approved Documents** view. Once the finance department completes payment, the status badge updates to **Ready for Payment** or **Settled**.

### Q6: What should I do if the AI misread an invoice total?
**Answer:** The AI provides a preview before you submit. If you notice a number was misread (for example, reading `₹1,000` as `₹1,800`), click directly into the text box and type the correct number. Once corrected, click **Confirm & Submit**.
